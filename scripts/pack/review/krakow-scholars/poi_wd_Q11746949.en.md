## teaser
The Bernardine Sisters of Poselska Street pray in this Baroque church of Saint Joseph.
It was built between 1694 and 1703, on the site of a demolished palace.
## full
You're standing at the Church of Saint Joseph, the convent church of the Bernardine Sisters on Poselska Street.
The present church dates from 1694 to 1703.
It was built where a palace of the Tęczyński family had been pulled down.
It is a Baroque church with a single nave and a barrel vault.

The city fire of 1850 partly burned the church, but luckily its interior survived.
The main altar, four side altars and the pulpit were made in the workshop of a Kraków carver between 1697 and 1699.

The main altar holds a painting of the church's patron with the boy Christ.
According to the convent's tradition, a bishop of Kraków received it from the pope in 1627.
He gave the image to his sister, a co-founder and the first superior of this convent of the Bernardine Sisters.
Since 1879, the feast of the church's patron has been held before this image, and the people of Kraków hold it in great devotion.
In 2016 the church became a diocesan sanctuary of its patron.

Before you walk on, look at the courtyard wall along the street, with its three small gates, designed in 1840.
